The Sisu Way style guide

Palette, type, mark, motif and components for thesisuway.com. Proposed by Summit Software Solutions; nothing here is final until Angus signs it off.

Idea

Fire in the cold

Sisu is a Finnish word, and Angus's story crosses the Alps, Siberia and Mongolia. The palette is a northern landscape at dusk: snow, granite, deep fjord water. One warm color, aurora, stands for the second wind, and it is spent only on the single action that matters on each screen.

Mark

Sun behind the ridge

A snow ridge line in front of a lower fjord ridge, with the aurora sun rising behind the peak: the moment the second wind arrives. The mark is a placeholder drawn for the concept; a designer should refine it once the name clears a trademark search.

The Sisu Way
The Sisu Way
  • Minimum size 24px for the mark, 120px wide for the lockup.
  • Clear space on every side equal to the height of the sun.
  • "Sisu" is the one word in the wordmark set in the brand color: fjord on light, aurora on dark.
  • Never recolor the sun, stretch the mark, or place it on aurora.
Motif

Contour lines

A journey drawn as elevation. Topographic contours sit behind dark sections at 22% and behind light sections at 14%. Never behind body text on a light section, never in color.

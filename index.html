The Sisu Way

Keep going. Find the second wind.

Sisu is the Finnish word for the quiet, stubborn resolve that keeps you moving when quitting would be easier. The Sisu Way turns it into something you can train.

Open the app Meet Angus
The word

Sisu is not motivation.

Motivation shows up when things are going well. Sisu is what is left when they are not.

Finns use it for the grit that carries a person past the point where the rational choice is to stop: extraordinary determination, courage, and the ability to keep acting when the odds say otherwise.

sisu (n.) Finnish. Strength of will and resilience in the face of adversity. The second wind that arrives after you thought you had nothing left.
The practice

Four ways to build it

Mindset

Name the voice that says stop, and learn to answer it. Short daily prompts that make resolve a habit, not a mood.

02

Endurance

Training that builds capacity slowly and on purpose, so there is something in reserve when it counts.

03

Recovery

Sleep, stillness and reflection. Resilience that lasts is built as much in the rest as in the effort.

04

Community

Nobody finds a second wind alone. Shared goals, honest check-ins and people who expect you to show up.

From the author

Angus Peacock is a rugby coach, coach educator, Royal Navy veteran and the author of SISU: A Series of Epic Adventures, a true account of turning personal trauma into a journey across Europe and Asia by rail and on foot, through the French Alps, Moscow, Beijing and Mongolia.

The Sisu Way is the practice behind that story, built for people who will never cross a steppe but still have to get up and keep going.
